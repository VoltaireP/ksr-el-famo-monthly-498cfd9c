October 2026. Arrows compare with the month before (September 2026).

This month at a glance

  • How things are going. October brought 6,495 conversations and 1,612 lead forms, against 7,207 and 1,798 the month before. Cost per conversation rose to $15.67 from $13.61, and cost per lead form to $63.15 from $54.57.
  • What to look into. Appointments read 875 against 569 the month before, but six new booking calendars are now counted for Audi, Kia and Mazda, so the two months are not on the same basis. Surf City costs the most per conversation at $25.09.
  • What is next. We recommend reviewing Surf City's ads and audience first, since it starts conversations at $25.09 against $15.67 overall, and comparing the Halloween campaigns with the regular ones before repeating them.
  • Creative ideas to consider. Every top ad is a Spanish VIP-appointment pitch that asks people to send a message. The Puente Hills video, headline 'Sí, ayúdame a conseguir un auto', lists ITIN, own business, first buyer and all credit, and led with 353 conversations at $10.89. Corona's good-credit version, 'Si Si, quiero obtener mi nuevo auto', brought 242 at $9.78, and Mazda's 'Si Si, quiero un auto nuevo' video 199 at $9.51. Worth testing the good-credit angle in Surf City, where conversations cost the most.
